How to make your tokens last

A token is a chunk of text, roughly ¾ of an English word. Models read and write in tokens, and limits on paid plans are counted in them too.

Start a new chat for a new topic

A long chat re-sends all earlier messages each turn. A fresh chat starts near zero.

Saves the whole history on every turn

Paste only the part that matters

Share the function, paragraph or table rows in question, not the entire file or document.

A 20-page PDF can be 15,000+ tokens

Edit your message instead of correcting

If the reply missed, edit and resend your prompt. A follow-up "no, I meant…" keeps the wrong answer in context.

Drops a wasted reply from history

Ask for the length you need

"Answer in 5 bullet points" or "code only, no explanation" keeps replies short. Output tokens often count more against limits.

Replies shrink 50–80%

Summarise, then restart

When the gauge turns amber, ask for a summary of decisions so far, then open a new chat with that summary.

Turns 50,000 tokens into ~800

Resize images before uploading

Image cost depends on pixel size. A screenshot cropped to the relevant area costs far less than a full screen.
